Guardrails, and what still gates approval

Held in all three

  • Workforce appears only as "thousands". No invented count of any kind, anywhere.
  • No clearance, FCL or NISPOM wording, per the agreed FSO review path.
  • No customer or shipyard names, and no identifiable customer imagery.
  • Newport News is closed and is not listed. Four branches plus the training facility.
  • Real photography only, all TSI's own, re-cropped and stripped of EXIF, GPS and XMP.
  • The training facility is promoted in all three and never named.
  • Fill time, the trade list and the review quote are shown as visible slots, not guesses. Each is a real thing TSI can supply or start measuring this month.
  • Every claim traces to a row in the content facts note.

Open inputs that can still move the winner

  • The named design decision maker. Asked in the room, never answered. Gates the design approval milestone.
  • The two or three websites Jay likes. Still the single biggest input into the first design round.
  • The trade list. The biggest AI-visibility lever on the page. NSC names real job titles on its homepage; TSI names none, and that is much of the 22/30 against their 30/30.
  • Call-to-crew time, show rate, retention. The three numbers that would make the proof section land. None are measured today.
  • Vector logo files. What is here is TSI's real artwork, lifted from the branding board PDF, but raster.
  • More photography. Only four frames in the batch are large and clean enough to carry a page.
  • Worker releases for the frames with identifiable faces, and pay ranges on job postings.
